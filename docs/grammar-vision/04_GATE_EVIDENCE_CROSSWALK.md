# Grammar Vision Gate Evidence Crosswalk

TO-Gn = Teaching OS quality gate for a teaching asset.
GV-Gn = Grammar Vision learner ability gate.

An asset cannot be released solely because a learner passes GV Gate, and a learner cannot advance solely because an asset passes TO Gate.

This crosswalk applies the ability ladder, six-dimensional evidence model, and quality Gates in [Teaching OS V2](../TEACHING_OS_V2.md) to the learner Gates in the [frozen Grammar Vision architecture](GRAMMAR_VISION_MVP_COURSE_ARCHITECTURE_V1.0_FROZEN.md). Status and evidence language follow the [Task 1 audit protocol](01_ADOPTION_AUDIT_PROTOCOL.md); current Grammar Vision delivery evidence is bounded by the [Task 2 adoption matrix](02_43_NODE_ADOPTION_MATRIX.md). It is an audit crosswalk, not a release claim.

| Grammar Vision learner Gate | Primary C0–C5 range | Required six-dimension evidence | Minimum TO Gate emphasis | One learner-visible proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GV-G1 Required Slot | C1–C2 | SEE + UNDERSTAND | TO-G2 Image, TO-G3 Logic, TO-G5 Camera | In a `Tom puts the ball ...` Scene, the learner points to the ball’s destination as a required `WHERE` Slot and chooses `in the box` to complete that Camera. |
| GV-G2 Core vs Layer | C1–C2 | SEE + UNDERSTAND; DISTINGUISH when Core/Layer confusion possible | TO-G2 Image, TO-G3 Logic, TO-G5 Camera | Given `Tom reads the book in the room` and `Tom puts the book in the room`, the learner folds the place layer in each Camera and explains why only the placement Scene loses a required part. |
| GV-G3 Mini Camera | C2–C3 | UNDERSTAND + DISTINGUISH + one justified TRANSFER | TO-G2 Image, TO-G4 Transfer, TO-G5 Camera | In the `my red ball` object Scene, the learner opens the `WHAT` Slot into a Target and Feature Mini Camera, then applies the same opening to a new `the small dog` Scene. |
| GV-G4 Relationship Prototype | C2–C3 | UNDERSTAND + DISTINGUISH + one justified TRANSFER | TO-G2 Image, TO-G4 Transfer, TO-G5 Camera | With a toy, table, and box Scene, the learner selects `at`, `on`, or `in`, explains the Target–Landmark Camera relation, and justifies the same relation in a new playground Scene. |
| GV-G5 Timeline View | C3 | UNDERSTAND + CREATE; HEAR when contextual audio supplied | TO-G3 Logic, TO-G5 Camera, TO-G7 Cognitive Load | After seeing one football Event, the learner chooses a Pattern Camera or an Inside-Event Camera and creates `Tom plays football` or `Tom is playing football` to match the shown moment. |
| GV-G6 Packaging | C3 | UNDERSTAND + CREATE; HEAR when contextual audio supplied | TO-G3 Logic, TO-G5 Camera, TO-G7 Cognitive Load | In a promise Scene, the learner packages `to put the ball in the box` as one Action Role inside `Mia wants ...`, then opens the package to show its internal Slots. |
| GV-G7 Sentence X-Ray | C4–C5 | READ reconstruction + CREATE re-expression + TRANSFER to a new Camera Tree | TO-G4 Transfer, TO-G6 Output, TO-G7 Cognitive Load | From a new longer sentence, the learner rebuilds the Camera Tree (Chunk → Scan → Focus → Lock → Fold → Rebuild), re-expresses the recovered Scene, and applies the process to a second unseen Camera Tree. |
