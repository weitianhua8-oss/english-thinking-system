## Audit questions per word

1. What modern sense should an 850 learner master first?
2. What historical claim is actually supported?
3. Which scene details are attested and which are reconstructed?
4. Is there a useful historical core scene?
5. Does it explain modern high-frequency meanings or merely entertain?
6. What existing mnemonic claims should be retained, downgraded to C/D, or rejected?
7. What should children see?
8. What optional deeper layer is useful for adults?
