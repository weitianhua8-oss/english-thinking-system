## Provisional S80 policy
Until the original tier list is recovered or deliberately replaced by a reviewed V2 list:
- do not label any reconstructed 80-word set as “the original S80”;
- use `status: confirmed-core` for historically evidenced items;
- use `status: candidate` for newly proposed S80 items;
- promote candidates only after semantic coverage, frequency, transfer value and curriculum role are reviewed.

## Selection criteria for a future reviewed S80 v2
A word deserves S80 priority when it does several of the following:
1. Generates many high-frequency meanings or constructions from one core image.
2. Combines productively with direction/preposition words.
3. Unlocks multiple sentence patterns.
4. Represents a basic cognitive relation: existence, state, movement, possession, transfer, perception, communication, space, time, causation or change.
5. Produces high learning transfer relative to the effort required.
6. Is especially prone to Chinese-translation misunderstanding and therefore benefits from English-thinking instruction.
